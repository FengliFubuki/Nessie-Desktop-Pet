# Nessie gaze mechanics

Best natural motion: the soft long neck bends and the small rounded head yaws/pitches toward attention; lower torso, belly, tail and resting flippers stay planted. This is a sticker-drawn organic plesiosaur, not a turntable. No whole-sprite rotation or tilt.

The original glossy black oval bead eyes have no white sclera. Preserve that construction and tiny highlights; redraw the visible eye shape with head angle, not new pupils or googly eye layers. The snout tip and neck bend carry gaze, eyelid shape supports pitch. Cream underside follows the neck continuously. Sparse scale marks follow surface, no new props.

Cardinal families in viewer coordinates:
- 000 up: neck upper curve reaches upward, snout clearly pitches toward top edge with cream chin exposed, visible eye looks up. Lower body unchanged.
- 090 screen-right: head/upper neck bend right, rounded nose tip unambiguously to right of head center, right-facing profile and one near eye; opposite eye occluded.
- 180 down: upper neck curls down with head center following the down-right family's physical neck arc; rounded snout pitches vertically toward bottom, crown visible, eye/eyelid lowered. This Nessie's soft neck has a naturally asymmetric forward curl. Head position may remain somewhat right of torso center at down; nose must clearly aim DOWN rather than toward either horizontal edge. This revised anchor avoids snapping a deep right curl to a tall left curl. Lower body unchanged and head scale preserved.
- 270 screen-left: upper neck/head bend left, nose tip unmistakably left of head center, opposing left profile and near-side eye; far eye occluded. Torso/tail/flippers must not flip.

Motion budget: smooth even approximately 22.5-degree increments of upper-neck bend and head aim, small follow-through at neck base. No abrupt body side flips, head size changes, anchor shifts or large isolated bends. 157.5→180 and 337.5→000 are each one ordinary step. Diagonals combine clear horizontal head bend with vertical snout pitch. Neutral is relaxed forward gaze, separate from up/down. Keep whole body contained with safe margins; no effects or shadows.
