# Nessie

Based on the user's three Nessie references: a soft green plesiosaur with cream underside, rounded long neck, glossy black bead eyes, subtle scales, dark olive outlines and a narrow white sticker rim. No costumes or props.

Generation used the built-in ImageGen tool. Canonical visual prompt: “A compact whole-body Apex Nessie in the supplied soft sticker/emote illustration style, lime olive green, cream belly, rounded snout, four flippers, short tail, sparse soft scale markings, dark olive outlines, gentle cel shading and narrow white rim; no props, text, shadows or detached effects.” A flat blue chroma background was removed by the deterministic sprite pipeline.

Each animation was generated as its own grounded strip: idle breathing/blink; screen-right alternating flipper gait; waving flipper; anticipation/lift/peak/descent/settle jump; sad droop/recovery; expectant asking pose; focused thinking/processing; focused review. Left movement mirrors each approved right-movement frame without reversing time order.

The gaze set uses a stable lower body and soft neck/head motion, with original bead eye construction. Four cardinal anchors ground two coherent eight-pose rows in clockwise 22.5-degree steps. Final directions are reviewed independently and in randomized blind axis pairs.

Package format: Codex pet v2, 192×208 cells, 8×11 atlas (1536×2288), lossless transparent WebP. Image strips are deterministically extracted, registered, assembled and cleaned with one final edge despill pass.
